# Design direction — Creative with a commercial mind

Black editorial foundation; acid yellow for evidence and contact, restrained cobalt for international perspective. Large tightly composed Inter headlines, regular-weight reading text, crisp rules and deliberately varied section proportions. Original photography carries human warmth. Avoid the rejected About gradient and city texture.

Reference interpretation: supplied Nike campaign boards inform assertive scale and selective color; supplied Ember boards inform spacious, legible pitch structure. Trevor Noah's official site informs immediate identity and direct content journeys (https://www.trevornoah.com/). Nike and Apple references inform focused hero communication and image-led evidence, not copied branding. Hape's fashion/culture positioning informs attitude; its client-rendered site was not visually verified. Awesome Design.md references are stored under docs/references. Refero was unavailable because the subscription endpoint returned NO_SUBSCRIPTION.

Motion: immediate readable hero, scroll-linked wave scale and title movement, staggered evidence reveals, responsive capability rows, restrained image zoom, consistent hover and focus feedback, animated navigation with user interruption. No multi-screen gate before identity/CTA. Reduced-motion users get all content without scroll motion. Mobile uses natural flow and no pinning.

Numbering: About 02, capabilities 03, work 04, experience 05, international background 06, contact 07; hero is opening 01. AI Lab is an unnumbered experimental interlude.

Validation: source syntax and existing server tests, desktop and mobile layout, bilingual rendering, filters, case dialog, navigation and reachable contact. Maintain source and backup independently.
